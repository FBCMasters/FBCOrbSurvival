# FBCOrbSurvival
Dodge, collect and fight in a glowing arena for 1-4 players

Red orbs are closing in. Collect yellow orbs, stay alive, and outlast everyone else.
Orb Survival is a fast arcade survival game for 1 to 4 players on a single keyboard. Play solo or with friends, in three different game modes, with optional bots that help (or compete with) you.

## GAME MODES
**Chase**
Red orbs hunt down the nearest living player. Keep moving, collect yellow orbs and use your abilities at the right moment.

**Random**
Red orbs fly in random directions and bounce off the arena walls. Less predictable, more chaotic.


**Shooter**
- Solo:
you stay in the center and aim with the mouse. Click to shoot the red orbs before they reach you. Enable bots to get five purple allies that use shields to protect you.
- Multiplayer:
all four players can move, and shooting automatically targets the nearest red orb. Red orbs spawn twice as often and move faster.

## FEATURES
- Solo and 4-player local multiplayer
- Solo modes are playable with mobile controls
- Three game modes
- Attack and heal abilities powered by yellow orbs
- Different bots, each with its own behavior: collectors, avoiders, seekers, and one that secretly follows other players
- Shield bots that take hits for you in Shooter mode
- Difficulty that keeps rising the longer you survive
- Full-screen effects, explosions, fireworks and an animated rainbow background
- Local high scores saved to a file on your computer

## CONTROLS
**Multiplayer (Chase / Random / Shooter)**
| Player | Move | Attack | Heal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | Arrow keys | Right Shift | Period (.) |
| Green | W A S D | E | Q |
| Pink | U H J K | I | Y |
| Orang | Mouse | Left click | Middle / Right click |

**Solo**
- Chase / Random: click anywhere to move. Reaching the point takes 1 second.
- Shooter: aim with the mouse and left click to shoot.

**Abilities**
- Attack: costs 10 yellow orbs, 20-second cooldown, destroys nearby red orbs.
- Heal: costs 20 yellow orbs, 40-second cooldown, restores 1 health (max 3).
- Press F8 to forfeit the current round.

## HIGH SCORES
To save your records, open HIGH SCORES from the main menu and select your data.txt file. Your browser will ask for read and write permission. You need to select the file again each time you reload the page.

## NOTES
- Best played in Chrome or Edge on desktop. High score saving relies on the File System Access API, which other browsers do not support.
- A keyboard is required for multiplayer.
- Sound is part of the experience, so turn your volume up. Good luck, and don't let the red ones catch you.
